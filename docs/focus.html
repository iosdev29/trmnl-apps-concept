Handling input with focus

How Next, Prev and Select reach your views on a touchbar and on a single button.

TRMNL UI 1.0 · App API v1 · TRMNL X · TRMNL OG

Actions, not gestures

Your app receives three actions. The firmware maps them to each device:

ActionTRMNL XTRMNL OG
Nexttap rightshort press
Prevtap leftdouble press
Selecttap middlehold

Hold middle (X), both corners, and the 5 s and 15 s holds belong to the system and never reach an app.

Who gets the press

The press goes down the tree and stops at the first view that uses it: a List(_:_:) or a Reader(_:_:). Titles, descriptions, spacers and bars ignore presses. If nothing uses the press, the screen stays as it is.

Put one focusable view on a screen. With two lists, the first one always wins.
