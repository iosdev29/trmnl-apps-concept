Screen updates and refresh

When the e-paper does a full refresh and when a fast partial one.

TRMNL UI 1.0 · App API v1 · TRMNL X · TRMNL OG

A full refresh flashes black and leaves a clean image; a partial one is fast and calm but can leave faint ghosts. TRMNL UI chooses for you:

SituationRefresh
The app opens, or its glance is drawnfull
The screen changed: its identity is differentfull
Anything else (focus moved, page turned)automatic: partial, with a full one every 8

A screen's identity is the text of its Title(_:) views. Switching from the library to a reader, or changing a title, counts as a new screen.

What a partial refresh covers

Not one component: the firmware draws the whole screen again in memory, compares it with the previous frame and refreshes one band of rows, from the first changed pixel row to the last. One changed Heatmap(_:_:_:_:) cell refreshes only its band; a changed line under the title plus changed numbers at the bottom refresh everything between them.

To keep a refresh small, change only what has to change on a press, and keep the parts that change together close to each other.
